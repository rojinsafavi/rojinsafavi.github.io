About

I am a Ph.D. in Bioinformatics with a strong emphasis on data science methodologies. My expertise lies in developing and applying machine learning models to analyze complex datasets, particularly in the healthcare domain. I have a proven track record of designing and implementing solutions for early cancer detection, biomarker discovery, and multi-omic data integration. My skills include proficiency in Python, R, and various machine learning frameworks, along with experience in data pipeline engineering and statistical modeling.

Research

My research focus lies at the intersection of bioinformatics and data science, driven by the application of machine learning and statistical modeling to unravel complex biological phenomena. I am particularly interested in leveraging large-scale omics datasets, including genomics, epigenomics (DNA methylation), and transcriptomics, to address critical challenges in early disease detection and personalized medicine. My work involves developing and implementing sophisticated analytical pipelines and machine learning algorithms, such as binomial mixture models, deep learning architectures (including transformers and VAEs), and multi-omic integration techniques, to identify subtle patterns and biomarkers indicative of disease states like urothelial carcinoma. A key aspect of my research involves the rigorous evaluation and validation of these data-driven models. This includes designing and executing analytical validation studies to assess crucial performance metrics like limit of detection and sensitivity, often employing statistical methodologies such as probit regression and sensitivity analysis. Furthermore, I am deeply involved in the integration of real-world evidence and electronic health records with omics data to enhance the translational potential of my research, aiming to bridge the gap between computational findings and clinical utility. My ultimate goal is to contribute to the development of more accurate, non-invasive diagnostic tools and personalized treatment strategies for various diseases.
